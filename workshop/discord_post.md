## Aotearoa v0.6.3 is out

**Designed by Keybro** · <https://keybrogaming.com>

**Kiwi tucker**
• L&P, Fresh Up, Foxton Fizz and Lift Plus cans
• Bluebird Ready Salted, Salt & Vinegar and Chicken, Munchos Tomato and Pineapple Lumps
• Griffin's Toffee Pops, Shrewsburys and Chocolate Thins
• Whittaker's Creamy Milk, Dark Ghana and Peanut Slab
• Wattie's beans, spaghetti and peaches, Sealord Tuna, Pams Sardines, Palm Corned Beef, Weet-Bix and Marmite
• They replace the vanilla food in the loot, and opened tins keep their label

**New Zealand loot**
• Hunting rifles, shotguns and .22/.308 ammo on the farms
• NZDF Steyr AUGs, M4s and FALs at Waiouru, Burnham, Linton and Ōhakea
• Handguns are rare; police stations have Glocks
• Mānuka honey, milk powder, gumboots, raincoats, jumpers and beanies
• Civilian towns top out at tier 3, so Palmerston North and Queenstown no longer spawn military loot in every house

**Fixed**
• Town and place names no longer overlap on the player map and the map item
• New Zealand weapons no longer spawn fully loaded, and ammo boxes no longer spawn full
