## 🇳🇿 Aotearoa v0.6.2 is out

**Designed by Keybro** · <https://keybrogaming.com>

**New**
• Keybro watermark on the in-game map screen, loading screens and map item
• Takistan pines and cycads in the bush and plantations, Livonia bunkers and buildings at the bases
• The world has its own navmesh settings, so the South Island and Stewart Island keep theirs

**Fixed**
• Warehouses, power poles, churches, tanks and trader fences now spawn (their class names were wrong)
• About 125,000 trees, rocks, guardrails and rail bridges pointed at models that don't exist
• No more Chernarus map overlay on the 2D map
• Linux servers no longer load the Chernarus gas zones
• Artillery sounds come from offshore, not over the towns

**Coming next: the New Zealand loot pass**
• Hunting rifles, shotguns and .22/.308 ammo on the farms
• NZDF Steyr AUGs, M4s and FALs at Waiouru, Burnham, Linton and Ōhakea
• Handguns are rare; police stations have Glocks
• Mānuka honey, milk powder, canned fish, gumboots, raincoats, jumpers and beanies
• Civilian towns top out at tier 3, so Palmerston North and Queenstown no longer spawn military loot in every house
