## Aotearoa v0.6.9 is out

**Designed by Keybro** · <https://keybrogaming.com>

**V Energy**
• V is in: a 500 ml can of the Kiwi guarana energy drink, in shops, schools and offices
• It has its own tall 500 ml can model on the way; until that's built it uses a V-labelled vanilla can

**Food stashes**
• 8 crashed Countdown trucks on the state highways, with food spilled round the trailer
• A stock room behind every dairy, and a pantry beside every bach
• Looted stashes slowly restock, every 45 minutes

**Kiwi tucker, done properly**
• Labels closer to the real thing: L&P's "World Famous in New Zealand", the Foxton windmill, "Kiwi kids are Weet-Bix kids"
• Food spawns where you'd expect it: snacks and fizzy in the shops, milk powder and honey on the farms, tinned fish on the coast
• 32 NZ food and drink items in all
